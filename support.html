Weekly Fork – Support

Need help or found a problem? Email weeklyforkapp@icloud.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

My timer did not alert me while Do Not Disturb was on. Open Settings, then Notifications, then Weekly Fork, and turn on Time Sensitive Notifications.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the Weekly Fork Pro screen (tap + on the Recipes tab after saving 3 recipes) and tap Restore purchase.

Is the allergy filter a safety guarantee? No. It matches ingredient names to help you filter recipes. Always read labels and follow your doctor's advice.
