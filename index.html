Weekly Fork – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Weekly Fork is a meal planning and grocery list app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Weekly Fork does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The following is saved only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage:

  • Your allergies and foods you dislike
  • Recipes you add
  • Your weekly plan and grocery list
  • Your settings, such as the number of people you cook for

We have no servers that receive this information, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Smart planning features

When your iPhone supports it, Weekly Fork can use Apple's on-device language model to interpret what you type in the planning box. This processing happens on your device. The text you type is not sent to us.

Purchases

The optional "Weekly Fork Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Sharing your grocery list

If you choose to share your grocery list, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet. The list goes only to the app and person you pick. We do not receive it.

Allergy information

Weekly Fork helps filter recipes and ingredients by matching ingredient names. It is a convenience tool, not a medical device or a safety guarantee. Always read ingredient labels and follow the advice of your doctor regarding allergies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Weekly Fork uses them only to tell you when a cooking timer you started has finished. These alerts are created on your device and no information is sent anywhere.
